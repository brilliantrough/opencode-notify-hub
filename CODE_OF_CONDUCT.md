# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in OpenCode Notify a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal or security-sensitive data.
- Accept maintainer decisions and correction in good faith.
- Help keep discussions welcoming and relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized language or imagery, trolling,
personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing another
person's private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact path
described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove content,
close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently
ban participants when behavior threatens the project or its community.

Reports will be reviewed as privately as practical. Retaliation against a
reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.
